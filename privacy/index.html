Z Academy privacy policy

Last updated 17 November 2026.

This Privacy Notice for Mansy Studio, located at Building 1, Degla Investment Area, Maadi, Cairo 11311, Egypt ('we', 'us', or 'our'), describes how and why we might access, collect, store, use, and/or share ('process') your personal information when you use our services ('Services'), including when you download and use our mobile applications Z Academy: Dental Exam Prep and Z Academy: Pharmacy Exam Prep (each, the 'App'), or any other application of ours that links to this Privacy Notice. Each App is run separately, with its own database and accounts.

Questions or concerns? Reading this Privacy Notice will help you understand your privacy rights and choices. If you do not agree with our policies and practices, please do not use our Services. If you still have any questions or concerns, please contact us at contact@ziyadmansy.com.

Summary of key points

  • The App works without an account. Your study progress is kept on your device.
  • An account is optional. If you sign in, we keep a copy of your progress and your Premium purchase so they work on your other devices.
  • Usage and crash data. We collect app usage events and crash reports to improve and fix the App; never your answers, question text, name or email. You can turn this off in Settings.
  • No sensitive information, no ads, no selling. We do not process sensitive personal information, show ads, collect the advertising id, or sell or share personal information for advertising.
  • Your rights. You can access, correct or delete your data, in the App or by email.

1. What information do we collect?

Information kept only on your device. Your settings, exam choice and date, answers, review schedule, bookmarks, study sessions and question reports are stored on your device and are not sent to us unless you sign in.

Personal information you give us when you sign in (optional). If you choose to sign in with Google (and, later, Apple), we receive your email address, your name and a unique user id from that provider. We then store a copy of your settings, bookmarks, answers, review schedule and study sessions, and a record of your Premium purchase (which plan, its start and end dates, the store's purchase reference and the store it was bought in).

Information collected automatically. When usage and crash data is on, the App sends a fixed list of usage events (for example that a practice set or a mock exam was finished, with the score only as a band such as '60-79%', or that the Premium screen was shown), crash reports (device model, operating system version, app version and the error), and a random app-installation id. It never sends question text, your answers, your name or your email address. The advertising id is not collected.

Purchases. Premium is bought through Google Play (and, later, the Apple App Store). The store processes the payment; we do not receive or store your payment details. We receive the purchase's plan, time and reference so we can unlock Premium.

Mock exam packs. When you start a mock exam from a pack you bought, the App stores in our database which of the pack's mock exams have been used, under a one-way code made from the store's purchase reference, so that used mock exams stay used after a reinstall. This record carries no account, name or email.

Question reports. When you report a question, the App sends to our database the question's id, the reason you chose, the option you had picked, where you reported it (practice or review), the exam, the app and content versions, your platform, a random reporter id kept on your device, and the time. A report carries no account, name, email or free text. A report made offline is sent later.

Messages you send us. When you write to us, the App opens your own email app; we receive whatever you choose to send.

2. How do we process your information?

We process your information to:

  • provide the App and its features, including Premium;
  • back up your progress and keep it in step across your devices, if you sign in;
  • make Premium available on all your devices, if you sign in;
  • understand how the App is used and fix crashes and errors;
  • respond to your reports and messages and correct questions;
  • prevent fraud and misuse, and comply with the law.

3. What legal bases do we rely on to process your information?

Where laws such as the GDPR or UK GDPR apply, we rely on: performance of a contract (providing the App, Premium and the optional account you ask for); legitimate interests (understanding usage and fixing crashes, which you can switch off at any time); consent where the law requires it; and legal obligations.

4. When and with whom do we share your personal information?

We do not sell or rent your personal information, and we do not share it for advertising. We use the following service providers, who process data on our behalf under their own terms and safeguards:

  • Google Firebase Authentication (sign-in), Cloud Firestore (account backup, question reports and mock exam pack records, stored in Europe), Firebase App Check (which checks, through Google Play Integrity, that requests come from the genuine App), Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics (usage events and crash reports) and Remote Config (app settings), provided by Google LLC;
  • Google Play (and, later, the Apple App Store), for purchases.

We may also disclose information if required by law, or in connection with a sale or transfer of the business, under the same protections.

5. How long do we keep your information?

Account data is kept until you delete your account; we then delete it from our database. Usage events are kept by Google Analytics for its standard retention period (14 months by default). Crash reports are kept by Crashlytics for 90 days. Emails you send us are kept as long as needed to deal with them.

6. How do we keep your information safe?

Data sent between the App and our service providers is encrypted in transit. Account data can be read or changed only by its owner, which our security rules enforce. The question bank in the App is encrypted. No method is completely secure, but we work to protect your information.

7. What are your privacy rights?

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or receive a copy of your personal information, to object to or restrict its processing, and to withdraw consent. You can use the App without an account, turn off usage and crash data in Settings, and delete your account in the App or by email (see Delete your account). To exercise any right, email contact@ziyadmansy.com. We will verify your identity and answer within 30 days. You may also complain to your local data protection authority.

8. Controls for do-not-track features

Most browsers and some mobile systems include a Do-Not-Track setting. No uniform standard for these signals exists, so we do not respond to them; instead, the App gives you its own switch for usage and crash data.

9. Do United States residents have specific privacy rights?

Residents of some US states have the rights to know, access, correct and delete their personal information and to appeal a decision about a request. We do not sell personal information or share it for targeted advertising. To make a request or appeal, email contact@ziyadmansy.com.

10. Do other regions have specific privacy rights?

If you live in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, Switzerland, Australia, New Zealand, South Africa, the Gulf states or elsewhere, you have the rights your local law gives you, which section 7 describes how to use. Your information may be processed outside your country, including in the European Union and the United States, with the safeguards our service providers apply.

11. Children's privacy

The Apps are for dentists, pharmacists and graduates of those professions preparing for licensing exams. They are not meant for anyone under 18, and we do not knowingly collect information from children. If you believe a child has given us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

12. Do we make updates to this notice?

Yes. We update this notice when the App changes how it handles information or when the law requires it, and change the 'Last updated' date. For significant changes, we will give notice in the App.
