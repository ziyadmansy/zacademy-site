Every Z Academy app · by Mansy Studio

Apps covered

This page applies to every Z Academy app.

  • Z Academy: Dental Exam Prep (Android: com.ziyadmansy.dentalexams), for dentists preparing for licensing exams.
  • Z Academy: Pharmacy Exam Prep (Android: com.ziyadmansy.pharmacyexams), for pharmacists preparing for licensing exams.

In the app

  1. Open the Z Academy app and tap the button at the top right of Today to open Settings.
  2. Tap Account, then Delete account, and confirm.

You may be asked to sign in again first, for your safety. Each app has its own account: deleting it in one app does not delete it in another, so delete it in each app you use.

Without the app

Email contact@ziyadmansy.com from the email address you signed in with, with the subject 'Delete my Z Academy account' and the name of each app to delete it in. We delete the account within 30 days and reply when it is done.

What is deleted

  • Your account: email address, name and user id.
  • Everything backed up to it: settings, bookmarks, answers, review schedule, study sessions and Premium purchase records.

Progress stored only on your device stays there until you uninstall the App. Usage and crash data is not linked to your account and is deleted on Google's standard schedule (see the Privacy Policy, section 5).

What is not affected

Deleting your account does not cancel or refund Premium. Premium bought on Google Play stays with your Google Play account and still unlocks the App on Android.
